Snapshot downloaded 2026-10-15. Original OSM XML, converted GeoJSON, metadata, license and conversion/network-generation methods are supplied with this distribution in MapData (Windows build) or Assets/TrajetoInclusivo/Data/Geography and Tools (Unity source project).

Alterations: rectangular extraction, road-edge clipping, conversion to GeoJSON, local UTM projection and generation of approximate offset sidewalks and hypothetical junction/access connections. Mapped footprint geometry retained where triangulation succeeds. Sidewalk widths, road widths inferred from lanes, building heights inferred from floors/defaults, elevations and accessibility conditions are simulation assumptions unless an original OSM tag is explicitly retained as an unverified source attribute.

The derived geographic database is provided under ODbL 1.0. This notice does not license the Unity engine or third-party software. See provenance.json for source, SHA-256 checksum, geographic origin, crop and processing details.

This is not a surveyed map, verified pedestrian itinerary or accessibility certification. The original synthetic testbed is separate and does not depict the actual location.
